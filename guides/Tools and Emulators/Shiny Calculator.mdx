<Gist>
  Check whether a Pokémon is shiny for a given TID/SID, or find the SID that
  makes a Pokémon shiny.
</Gist>

This calculator works for Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Diamond, Pearl, and Platinum.

## Check if a Pokémon is shiny

Enter your TID, SID, and the Pokémon's PID. The calculator will show
whether that Pokémon is shiny for the given trainer ID pair.

<IsPokemonShinyForTidSid />

## Find a SID that makes a Pokémon shiny

Enter your TID and the Pokémon's PID. The calculator will output all eight SIDs
that make the Pokémon shiny, along with their shared TSV and the matching PSV.

The results list one square shiny SID and seven star shiny SIDs. Square and star
animations are only distinguished in Gen 8 and later; all eight SIDs produce a
shiny Pokémon in the games covered here.
