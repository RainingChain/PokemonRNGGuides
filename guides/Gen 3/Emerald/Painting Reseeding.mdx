## Prerequisites

- Access to the Battle Frontier.
- Owning a Egg Pokémon and a Pokémon knowing Sweet Scent.
- Recommended: A Master Ball or a Pokémon knowing False Swipe.
- Recommended: 5+ Rare Candies.
- Know how to create a [Battle Video](/emerald-battle-video).
- Recommended: Having read [Overview of RNG Advancing Techniques](/emerald-advancing-rng-techniques).

## General Overview

Painting Reseeding is a RNG technique to make the RNG state jump by millions of advances instantly, giving access to
RNG states that would normally not be reachable realistically.

By interacting with a painting in Lilycove contest hall, the RNG state is reseeded using the current visual frame counter. Players don't need to have participated in a contest for the paintings to be accessible.

This technique requires a frame-perfect input. Players need to validate whether they successfully did the technique by catching a high-level Pokémon.

### Steps Overview

1. Determine the target frame before painting and advance after painting.
1. Interact with the painting, create a Battle Video and validate whether the target frame before painting was hit.
1. Update the Battle Video.


## Webtool and Instructions

1. Save in front of a painting in the Lilycove contest hall, with 3 Pokémon eligible for Battle Tower, and one knowing Fly.
1. On the website, start the timers.
1. When the first timer reaches 0, press `START+SELECT+A+B` to soft reset the game.
1. Select your save file and wait while standing in the front of the painting.
1. When the 2nd timer reaches 0, press `A` to interact with the painting.
1. Close the painting and quickly leave the contest hall.
1. Fly to Battle Frontier and quickly reach the Battle Tower by running.
1. NO_PROD copy from battle vide

1. Save the game.
1. Place any Egg Pokémon at the first slot of your party. Withdraw a Pokémon knowing Sweet Scent. Ideally, if you don't own a Master Ball, place a Pokémon knowing False Swipe at the 2nd slot of the party.
1. Fly to the Victory Road NO_PROD map name. and enter Victory Road cave. Move 1 step away from the entrance, otherwise Sweet Scent will fail.
1. TODO battel vide
1. Catch the Pokémon.
1. Open the Pokémon summary and fill the fields on the website with the Pokémon stats. Press "Generate".
1. If there's a result matching the target advance before painting, click the "Go Next Step" button and ignore the rest of the steps.
1. If there are no results or if none of them have a High or higher confidence rating, ensure that the content of the fields are correct.
1. If there are multiple results with a Very High confidence rating (or multiple High ratings):
    1. On the website, set the Rare Candy count to 1, and give a Rare Candy to your Pokémon.
    1. On the website, remove Pokémon entries with stats that don't match the newly improved stats of the Pokémon.
    1. Repeat the previous steps to feed the Pokémon additional Rare Candies and filter the entries until there's only one Very High confidence rating left (or only one High rating).
    1. Additionnaly, you can fly to the Battle Frontier, and talk with the stat judge. On the website, remove Pokémon entries that don't match what the stat judge says.
1. If there is only one result with a Very High confidence rating (or only one High rating), click on its "Calibrate" button.
1. This painting attempt was not successful, but the timers were adjusted to improve the odds for your next attempt. Go back to step 2.


## Technical Info
- The RNG state before interacting with the painting has no impact on the new RNG state. It is not possible to use Battle Video to speedup the wait before interacting with the painting.
- The Egg Pokémon lead ensures that the caught Pokémon will be generated with [method Wild1](/gba-methods-lead-impact).
