### Practical Applications of RNG Manipulation

- Obtaining Pokémon with rare traits (ex: Shiny, perfect IVs, Nature, Ability):
  - Static Pokémon (Easiest to RNG, only way to RNG most Legendary Pokémon).
  - [Wild Pokémon](/retail-emerald-wild) (Only way to RNG Ditto).
  - [Egg Pokémon](/retail-emerald-egg) (Only way for most perfect Pokémon).
- [Accessing Mirage Island](/emerald-mirage-island) (Only way to get Liechi Berry).
- [Predicting Battle Frontier](https://pokemoncompletion.com/BattleFacilities/Emerald/RngManipulation) teams for easy Gold Symbols.
- [Getting Pokérus](/emerald-pokerus-emu), which is notably required to get optimal low-level Pokémon such as Level 5 Smeargle with Dragon Rage.
- Getting big Lotad and Seedot to show to brothers in Sootopolis City.

### RNG Manipulation Techniques

- [Finding your SID](/gen3-sid), which is required to RNG manipulate a shiny Pokémon.
- Change your TID/SID with Arbitrary code execution (ACE), to make a specific Pokémon shiny.
- Shortening the wait time to reach high RNG advances:
  - Make the RNG state jump by millions of advances instantly with [Painting Reseeding](/emerald-painting-reseeding).
  - Saving and restoring RNG advances with [Battle Video](/emerald-battle-video).
- Control what [method](/gba-methods) used to generate Pokémon in wild encounters, by choosing an [optimal lead](/gba-methods-lead-impact).
- Determining the PID of a Pokémon from its IVs.
